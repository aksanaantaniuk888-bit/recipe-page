Homemade Spaghetti with Meatballs

This homemade spaghetti with meatballs recipe is a delicious and comforting Italian-inspired dish. It combines tender meatballs, rich tomato sauce, and spaghetti topped with Parmesan cheese.

Recipe information

Detail Information
Preparation time 20 min
Cooking time 35 min
Total time 55 min
Difficilty Easy

Ingredients

Spaghetti

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 50 g Parmesan cheese

Meatballs

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 garlic cloves

Tomato sause

  • 400 g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Cooking instructions

    Cooking the meatballs

  1. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, egg, breadcrumbs, finely chopped onion, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Gently mix the ingredients and shape the mixture into small, round meatballs.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the meatballs and cook, turning occasionally, until browned and fully cooked.
  4. Prepare the tomato sauce
  5. Boil the spagethetti. in saltedd water.
  6. Mix the spagethetti with. sauce and serve with meatballs.
  7. Serve with parmesan cheese
