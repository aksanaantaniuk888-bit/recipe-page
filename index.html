Homemade Spaghetti with Meatballs

This homemade spaghetti with meatballs is a comforting Italian-inspired meal, perfect for a family dinner. It combines juicy beef meatballs, spaghetti, and a rich tomato sauce made with garlic and herbs. The dish is topped with Parmesan cheese and serves four people.

Preparation time 20 min
Cooking time 35 min
Total time 55 min
Difficilty Easy

Ingredients

Spaghetti

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 50 g Parmesan cheese

Meatballs

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 garlic cloves

Tomato sause

  • 400 g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Cooking instructions

Cooking the meatballs

  1. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, egg, breadcrumbs, finely chopped onion, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Gently mix the ingredients and shape the mixture into small, round meatballs.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the meatballs and cook, turning occasionally, until browned and fully cooked.

Tomato sause

  1. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute.
  2. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and dried basil. Stir everything together until well combined.
  3. Reduce the heat and let the sauce simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Cooking spaghetti

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain the spaghetti.
  2. Add the cooked spaghetti to the tomato sauce and gently mix until the pasta is evenly coated.

Serve with the meatballs and sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top.
